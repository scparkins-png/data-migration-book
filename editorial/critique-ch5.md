# Critical Edit — Chapter 5: The Preparation Work

26 October 2026 · branch `critical-edit-ch5` · checked against the Step 5 template (Canva) and the Excel checklist

## Verdict

This is the most "doing" chapter in the book, and its teaching is excellent. The strongest parts are:
- the five-column sheet;
- the mismatch types (and they match your template's help notes);
- the "picklists first" warning;
- the vague-vs-good cleaning rules;
- the five-row worked example.

The structural problem is that **every lesson is taught twice**. The subsections teach real samples, picklists, precise rules, documents and sample testing, and then Key things re-teaches the same five in full paragraphs. Words: about 3,700 → 2,560. That's the biggest saving of any chapter.

## Proposals

**P1 — Hook without a signature.** "This is my favourite bit: taking messy, scattered data and making it work properly." It's your book, so signing your own quote "— Stuart Parkins" reads oddly. This settles decision D7 for this chapter.

**P2 — Where You Are: two sentences** instead of a recap of every earlier chapter. "If the earlier chapters were about understanding, this one is about **doing**" is kept.

**P3 — Key things becomes a summary, not a repeat.** The subsections stay as the teaching. Key things shrinks to a five-line checklist of the same rules, led by your structural point: "0. Get the structure right before the fields". That was tucked at the end before; it's the first thing to get right, so it now goes first.

**P4 — Tighter subsections.**
- The mismatch types become a bulleted list.
- The vague-vs-good cleaning rules become a two-column table.
- "Do not forget documents" joins the mismatch list as the last item.
- Nothing taught is lost.

**P5 — From the Field in first person.** The third-person opening line ("documented by Stuart Parkins") goes. "In my own words at the time" becomes your words in the text. "Column B / Column K" becomes "one column … another", since the letters mean nothing to a reader.

**P6 — "That's people for you" said once in the book.** It already appears in Chapter 3. Here it becomes "the same team habit from Step 3 showed up again", which links back rather than repeating.

**P7 — Checklist: 12 items → 6 essentials**, tagged 5A–5C. They match the Excel checklist references.

**P8 — Gate: 8 → 6.** The sample-load, rules-updated and test-data rows are merged.

**P9 — Your Turn → Exercises 5.1 and 5.2** as questions; the tables move to Worksheets 5.1 and 5.2.

**P10 — Download link fixed.** "spdata.co.uk" becomes **stuartparkins.com**, like every other chapter (edit-log X8). The template name, *Simple Mapping Sheet*, matches your Canva design.

**P11 — Small fixes:** "SSMS" is spelled out as "SQL Server" in What you'll need; "10 %" → "10%"; "an artefact" → "a document".
