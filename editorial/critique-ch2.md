# Critical Edit — Chapter 2: Is Your Data Ready?

25 October 2026 · branch `critical-edit-ch2` · same pattern as Chapter 1

## Verdict

This is the most useful chapter in the book for a non-technical reader. The seven dimensions give people a way to measure what they would otherwise only worry about. Three things are the chapter's strongest content:
- the completeness-vs-accuracy box
- "better shape than they fear and worse than they assume"
- the story of timing the clean-up to win over management

The problem is that **the case study is told three times**:
1. in Why This Matters
2. in From the Field, as three bullet lists
3. in the worked example table

On top of that, the checklist has 14 items under three intro paragraphs, and there are three separate closings after the gate. Words: 3,450 → about 2,370.
