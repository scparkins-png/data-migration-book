## Keeping your files together

Keep one folder — digital or physical — for the whole migration, with a sub-folder for each step. Save each completed template and worksheet into it as you go. By go-live, that folder is the record of what you moved, why, and how you proved it worked; it is also what the business keeps when the project team steps back.
