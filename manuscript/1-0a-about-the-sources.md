# About the Sources

Unless cited otherwise, the framework, templates, help notes, project plan checklist and the running Sage 50 → Sage X3 case study referenced throughout this handbook are my own work. Where third-party material is used — particularly the Forbes and McKinsey research on migration overruns referred to in the Introduction — that source carries a numbered note and a full entry in the References appendix.

The case study running through every chapter is drawn from my published article *A Case Study from a Sage 50 to Sage X3 Migration* (stuartparkins.com). Where the chapters quote directly from real notes or settings from that project, the source is named inline rather than footnoted.

The downloadable templates that accompany this handbook — the Project Plan Checklist, the Step 1 to Step 6 sheets, and the supporting reference cards — are available at stuartparkins.com.
