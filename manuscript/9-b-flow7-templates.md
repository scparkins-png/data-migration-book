# Appendix B — The Flow7 Templates

*Every Flow7 download, by step — all at **stuartparkins.com***

The handbook tells you *what* to do and *why*. These files are where you do it. There are three kinds:

| Download | What it's for | Format |
|:-|:-|:-|
| **The Project Plan Checklist** | The master tracker — every task in Steps 1 to 7, with status, owner and date columns | Excel (and PDF) |
| **The step templates** | One set per step, for the real work: inventories, assessments, mapping sheets, test scripts | PDF |
| **The Flow7 Migration Workbook** | All the step templates in one Excel file, if you prefer to work in a spreadsheet | Excel |

## Keeping your files together

Keep one folder — digital or physical — for the whole migration, with a sub-folder for each step. Save each completed template into it as you go, along with your notes. By go-live, that folder is the record of what you moved, why, and how you proved it worked; it is also what the business keeps when the project team steps back.
